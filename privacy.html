Privacy Policy

Exhibit Stamp does not collect, transmit, or store any data about you or your documents.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no personal data, no usage data, no diagnostic data, no crash reporting, and no analytics of any kind. No information about you, your Mac, or the documents you mark is gathered.

What the app transmits

Nothing. Exhibit Stamp works entirely offline. It is distributed as a sandboxed application without the network entitlement, which means the operating system itself prevents it from making a network connection. This is not a statement of intent; it is a property of the app that can be inspected.

Your PDFs and images are read from the files and folders you choose, and the stamped exhibits and the exhibit index are written where you choose. They go nowhere else. There is no cloud service, no upload, no sync.

The originals are never modified. Exporting always writes new files, and an export never replaces an existing file.

Purchases

The app is free to try. Exhibit Stamp Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by macOS and the App Store. The app asks the system whether Pro has been bought and receives a yes or a no; it never sees your name, your Apple Account or any payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

Accounts

There are none. Exhibit Stamp has no sign-up, no login, no licence server and no activation.

What the app stores on your Mac

Only what makes the app useful the next time you open it, all of it local:

Your saved sticker templates
The templates you save — colours, border, font, size and fields. Held in the app's own preferences, on your Mac.
A reference to your last output folder
A security-scoped bookmark, so the folder chooser opens where you were last time. It grants access to that folder only, and only to this app.
Project files you save
An .exhibitstamp file holds the list of exhibits, their numbers and descriptions, and your settings, with references to the original files so they can be found again. It is saved where you choose and never leaves your Mac unless you move it.

Passwords for protected PDFs are held in memory while the window is open and are never written anywhere, not even in the project file.

Third parties

There are none. Exhibit Stamp uses no third-party frameworks, no software development kits, no advertising, and no external services. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Children

The app is a professional document tool and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page. An app update that started collecting data would be a different app, and would say so plainly here first.
